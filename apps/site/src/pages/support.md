For anything not covered here, email [support@lingercam.app](mailto:support@lingercam.app). Include
the event name, and if you're a host, the email you sign in with.

## Get the app

Hosts create and manage events in the Linger app for iPhone and Android. The store listings aren't
live yet; this page will link to them once they are. Guests don't need the app: they join from the
event's QR code or link in their browser.

## Hosting

### How do I start an event?

Open the app, sign in with your email and tap to create an event. Pick the occasion, name it, set
when photos appear and how many shots each guest gets, choose a look and a guest count. Then print
the table cards or share the link.

### Can I change the settings after the event starts?

The title, end time, reveal time and who sees the photos can still change while the event is live.
The film look and shots per guest are fixed once the event goes live, so every photo matches and
every guest has the same number of shots.

### Why can't I see the photos yet?

Nobody sees the photos before the reveal, the host included. They appear for everyone at the reveal
time you chose.

### How do I remove a photo?

As the host, open the photo and hide it. A hidden photo disappears for everyone at the event.

## Guests

### The camera won't open

Check that your browser has permission to use the camera. On iPhone that's in Settings, under
Safari, Camera. On Android it's in the browser's site settings. Then reload the page.

### I ran out of shots

The host sets the number of shots per guest. They can't be topped up by guests.

### My photos haven't uploaded

Photos upload in the background and retry when the connection comes back. Keep the page or app open
for a moment once you have signal.

## Payments

Small events are free, and larger events are a single payment by guest count; see
[Pricing](/pricing/) for the sizes and prices. Purchases made in the app are handled by the App Store or Google Play, and
web purchases by Stripe. For a problem with a payment, email us with the event name.

## Your account

You can delete your account and everything in it from the app under Settings, or by email. See
[Delete your account](/delete-account/).
